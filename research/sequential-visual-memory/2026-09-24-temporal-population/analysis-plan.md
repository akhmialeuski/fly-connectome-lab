# Frozen stage-0 response analysis

This analysis plan was written before the first real T27 response run. It adds explicit descriptive thresholds to the already committed protocol without changing the cohort, masks, conditions, or advancement rules.

- For each of the 24 `(sample_id, raster window)` units, pair C1-C4 with C0 and C5 with C6 at the same global physical step and neuron index. A response after stimulus offset is compared with the blank at that global step. Keep stimulus-offset time separate from global time.
- At each checkpoint and population, report the mean and distribution of `mean(abs(voltage_condition - voltage_blank))`, `sqrt(mean((voltage_condition - voltage_blank)^2))`, and the signed mean difference across the 1,314 measured cells. Use float64 accumulation on saved float32 values. Report each image/window pair as well as population aggregates; no classifier is fitted in stage 0.
- A neuron is **silent over a stimulus** if its cumulative spike count at the stimulus endpoint is zero. A neuron is **high-rate** if it fires on at least half the stimulus steps (at least 5/10 or 10/20). A checkpoint voltage is **near threshold** when `0.9 <= v < 1.0` after the normal spike reset. These are descriptive thresholds fixed before viewing responses; they are not biological labels or success gates.
- Report per-step total and active-neuron counts and episode-noise kicks. Validate exact equality of the noise-kick sequence for C0-C4 over their shared simulated steps, zero kicks in C5/C6, zero encoder input in C0/C6, and C1 terminal parity with the existing runtime. Any failure invalidates the affected comparison and remains in the attempt ledger.
- For each window and population, report the variance of response vectors across the eight training photographs, with the explicit caveat that only four identities and two photographs each were sampled. Do not use a same-identity score, validation accuracy, or a best-population ranking as a pilot result.
- The pilot advances to a separately frozen 20-identity training-only expansion only if all source/parity/noise controls pass and at least one population has a finite nonzero paired C1 evoked RMS voltage response at the stimulus endpoint. If every population is numerically zero, inspect encoder delivery and recording before expanding. A nonzero response is necessary for a signal-delivery map but is not evidence of identity separability.
